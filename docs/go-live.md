# Go-live checklist (US-032, plan M11 item 4)

Everything that could not be done without the staging VM, the domain, the real credentials and the real services, collected from the stories' staging follow-ups, the runbooks and the plan, plus the plan's own go-live list. Nothing here is done yet: staging and production do not exist (US-014, US-015 are blocked on the Owner).

- **Owner** = the person who owns the business and holds the credentials (admin console as Owner, Midtrans, WAHA, DNS registrar, escrow). **Operator** = whoever has root on the VM and the CI settings.
- Every item is one line: a checkbox, an id (`GL-nn`, cited by [ac-traceability.md](ac-traceability.md)), what to do, who, how to verify, and the source. Tick it only with the evidence the Verify step names, and write the date and the result next to the box.
- `infra/verify/src/ac-traceability.test.ts` checks this file: unique ids, an owner and a verify step on every item, and every `GL-nn` that the traceability table cites exists here.
- Order: A to C can run in parallel once staging exists; D after C; E needs A to D; F is the production switch and needs all of them.
